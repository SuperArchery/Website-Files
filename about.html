What Is Theatre Xavier?

Theatre Xavier is a place where students can learn how to act in a character, run the stage, make goofy costumes, tweak some lights, or amplify the sound!

What is the outcome of Theatre Xavier?

At Theatre Xavier, we help students find the thing that helps them find their voice and learn to develop healthy, long-lasting friendships with the people they work with.

How often do we produce shows?

We produce two shows a year; typically, we produce a comedy or play in the fall. In the spring, we produce a musical, and once in a while, we might produce a third show or interactive performance in January.

Who can sign up?

Any high school student from any school can sign up for Theatre Xavier! We have approximately 70–100 students participate in Theatre Xavier, with 80% of participants being male.

How can I sign up for Theatre Xavier?

To sign up for Theatre Xavier, visit theatrexavier.ludus.com and tap "Register for TX". Then, complete the form to sign up.

Make sure you also sign the TX Handbook at stxavier.org/txhandbook to read expectations for Theatre Xavier.
